THE SOUND OF LEBANON

Lebanese Music

Music is an important part of Lebanese culture. From traditional instruments and Dabke to modern pop, Lebanese music has developed into a diverse and recognizable sound.

🎤

Fairuz

One of the most iconic voices in the Arab world, Fairuz's music has become deeply connected with Lebanon.

🎙️

Sabah

A legendary Lebanese singer and actress whose career spanned many decades.

🎶

Wadih El Safi

A celebrated Lebanese singer known for his powerful voice and contribution to traditional Arabic music.

🥁

Dabke

A traditional Levantine dance usually performed with rhythmic music during celebrations.

🪕

The Oud

A traditional string instrument that plays an important role in Arabic and Lebanese music.

🎧

Modern Music

Today's Lebanese artists combine traditional influences with pop, rock, hip-hop, electronic music, and more.

Music carries the stories of a people.

Traditional roots. Modern sounds. Lebanese identity.
