What to Eat in Lebanon

Lebanese cuisine is famous for its fresh ingredients, colorful dishes, spices, and variety. Here are some foods you should try.

🥙

Shawarma

Thinly sliced seasoned meat served in bread with vegetables, pickles, and sauces.

🥗

Tabbouleh

A fresh salad made with parsley, tomatoes, bulgur, mint, lemon, and olive oil.

🧆

Falafel

Crispy fried balls made from ground chickpeas and herbs.

🥩

Kibbeh

A traditional dish made with bulgur, minced meat, onions, and spices.

🫓

Manakish

A popular Lebanese flatbread topped with za'atar, cheese, or minced meat.

🍰

Knafeh

A traditional dessert made with cheese, pastry, and sweet syrup.
